# Account

Achordeon runs without one. Writing songs, binding songbooks, performing them on
stage: all of it works with no account at all, because your library lives in the
browser on this device. The account exists for one thing, getting that library
off this one device.

Without it you can still back up by hand, to a file you keep yourself. With it,
the backup can go to your Google Drive instead, and on the paid tier the library
follows you between devices on its own.

## What a login gives you

The application is fully offline. You don't need to login. But login will give you more functionality depending on you tier:

- **No login**
  - manual synchronization via file download & upload
- **Free**
  - manual synchronization of the profile to the google drive
- **Premium**
  - same as Free tier
  - your files can be automatically synchronized to our servers
  - you can have `Audience`

:::info[Testing]
Audience can be for free tier as well
:::

Application supports only one user in the browser. Everyone can see your data. The login is for synchronization only.

## What needs a connection

"Fully offline" has exceptions, and every one of them is something that involves
somebody else's computer:

- Signing in and registering
- Automatic sync - it queues and catches up later, and the row reads **Some
  changes have not reached the cloud yet** in the meantime
- Google Drive, in both directions
- Adding a font. Once a font is on the device it is never fetched again
- Hosting or joining an audience lobby. Performing from the stage yourself is not
- Reporting a bug, and the buttons beside it that open this site
- Checking for a new version, which fails quietly

Nothing else - not the editor, not the renderer, not a PDF, not a songbook, not a
performance, not an import, not a backup to a file and not a restore from one.

:::tip[Open a song in each title font once before you go]

Achordeon ships four fonts, and only Roboto Mono - the one the app itself is set
in - is copied down at install time. Caveat, Crimson Text and Oswald are fetched
the first time a page needs one, so a font you have never rendered may not be
there when you are offline.

:::

## Signing in

- With Google, or with an email and a password
- A second sign-in method can be added to the same account later, and then either
  one gets you in

Signing in with Google does not hand over your Drive. That is a separate
permission, asked the first time you back up to Drive. What Google tells us about
you, what Achordeon can touch in your Drive, and how to delete the account, are
all on [your account & Google data](/settings-account/account-data.mdx).

## One browser, one library

The first sign-in claims the library on this device. From then on those songs
belong to that account:

- Sign out and the lists go empty. The songs are hidden, not deleted, and signing
  back in brings them straight back
- Sign in as somebody else in the same browser and you get told so - **These
  songs belong to another account** - with the choice of signing in as the owner
  instead, or **Use this account's library**, which clears this device's copy of
  their library and puts yours here

One browser holds one library, and it belongs to one account.

## Where it is in the app

The account lives in one place, [Settings → Profile](/settings-account/settings.mdx#profile).
The backup buttons it unlocks sit a little further down the same page, under
[Backup](/settings-account/settings.mdx#backup).
