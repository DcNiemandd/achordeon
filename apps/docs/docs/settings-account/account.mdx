# Account

Achordeon runs without one. Writing songs, binding songbooks, performing them on
stage: all of it works with no account at all, because your library lives in the
browser on this device. The account exists for one thing, getting that library
off this one device.

Without it you can still back up by hand, to a file you keep yourself. With it,
the backup can go to your Google Drive instead, and on the paid tier the library
follows you between devices on its own.

## What a login gives you

The application is fully offline. You don't need to login. But login will give you more functionality depending on you tier:

- **No login**
  - manual synchronization via file download & upload
- **Free**
  - manual synchronization of the profile to the google drive
- **Premium**
  - same as Free tier
  - your files can be automatically synchronized to our servers
  - you can have `Audience`

:::info[Testing]
Audience can be for free tier as well
:::

Application supports only one user in the browser. Everyone can see your data. The login is for synchronization only.

## Signing in

- With Google, or with an email and a password
- A second sign-in method can be added to the same account later, and then either
  one gets you in

Signing in with Google does not hand over your Drive. That is a separate
permission, asked the first time you back up to Drive. What Google tells us about
you, what Achordeon can touch in your Drive, and how to delete the account, are
all on [your account & Google data](/settings-account/account-data.mdx).

## Where it is in the app

The account lives in one place, [Settings → Profile](/settings-account/settings.mdx#profile).
The backup buttons it unlocks sit a little further down the same page, under
[Backup](/settings-account/settings.mdx#backup).
