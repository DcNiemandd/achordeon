# Patch notes

Achordeon updates itself in the background and tells you when a new version is
waiting - this is the page that says what you would be reloading into.

## 2026-08-04

- **Rotate wide songs** - a song wider than it is tall gets a quarter turn, so it
  fills the screen instead of a strip across the middle of it. The performing menu
  has a **Rotate wide songs** row on phones and tablets, on stage and in the
  audience alike, and it only shows up on a song that would gain by it - portrait
  songs in the same setlist stay upright. A downloaded sheet turns on its own,
  nothing to set. [Stage and audience](/stage-audience/index.mdx)
- **Match this screen, sideways** - a new aspect ratio preset that writes the shape
  of your screen laid on its side, for when the device is locked to portrait and
  turning it changes nothing. [Rendering](/settings.mdx#rendering)
- **Lists come back where you left them** - opening a song or a songbook and
  pressing back returns you to the row you were on, not the top of a list you had
  scrolled a long way down. [Songs](/songs/index.mdx#list)
- **Transpose while performing** - play a song in another key without changing
  the song. The performing menu has a **Transpose** row; on a desktop the control
  sits in the bar. The audience has one of its own, so a viewer with a capo is not
  bound to what the performer is playing. [Transposing](/stage-audience/index.mdx#transposing)
