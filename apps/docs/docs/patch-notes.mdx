# Patch notes

What changed, newest first. Achordeon updates itself in the background and tells
you when a new version is waiting - this is the page that says what you would be
reloading into.

There are no version numbers. Nothing here is released in the usual sense: the app
ships when something is finished, so a date is the honest label.

## 2026-08-04

**Transposing on stage and in the audience.** You can now play a song in a
different key without touching the song. The performing menu has a **Transpose**
row (on a desktop the arrows sit in the bar itself), and the audience has the same
control - a viewer with a capo is not bound to whatever the performer is playing.

The offset holds for the whole performance and disappears when you exit. Nothing is
saved and nothing is sent: your song keeps the chords you wrote, and the audience
keeps its own key. For a change that stays, transpose in the editor instead.

See [Transposing](/stage-audience/index.mdx#transposing).
